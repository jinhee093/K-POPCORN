# K-Popcorn Uploader

K-Popcorn Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the K-Popcorn YouTube channel to upload the channel's own Shorts videos through the YouTube Data API.

## Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

The tool is used only by the channel owner. It is not offered to other users.

It requests one permission: uploading videos to the owner's YouTube channel (youtube.upload).

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sent to any server other than Google and YouTube.

No personal data of other people is collected, stored, sold or shared.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
